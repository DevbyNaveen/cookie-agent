GPU research for a full month with at most $5 cash
Checked: 2026-10-31. Public-source research only; no account enrollment,
credit grant verification, payment, deployment, or GPU allocation performed.

Finding
Recurring free quotas and credits can support research sessions throughout a
month within this cash budget. No suitable guaranteed 24/7 dedicated GPU at
$5/month was verified. Earlier advice overemphasized paid hourly rentals and
missed the stronger recurring-credit route. Provider availability and account
eligibility still need confirmation. Research on another provider does not
change the user's preference for an eventual AWS production deployment.

1. Modal Starter — first recommendation for configurable benchmark jobs
Published plan: $0 subscription plus metered usage, $30 compute credits monthly.
L4 is $0.000222/s; L40S is $0.000542/s. CPU costs $0.0000131/core/s and RAM
$0.00000222/GiB/s. Load time and a configurable idle tail are billable. Use custom
Functions, not paid-per-token Shared Endpoints, which are outside included compute.
Illustrative calculations, not reservations or measured capacity:
L4 + 2 physical CPU cores + 16 GiB RAM = $1.021392/hour -> about 29.37 hours/$30.
L40S + 2 physical CPU cores + 32 GiB RAM = $2.301264/hour -> about 13.04 hours/$30.
Actual requested/used resources and region selection can increase costs. These
are alternative uses of the same credit pool, not additive allowances. Setup
time consumes that allowance. Do not assume a large model's loading RAM fits
the example. Volumes have an included 1 TiB allowance on the published page.
Sources: https://modal.com/pricing
https://modal.com/docs/guide/gpu

Modal billing protection
A payment method is required. Workspace usage budgets are BEFORE credits; the
separate Workspace spend limit caps net out-of-pocket charges AFTER credits.
The default net limit may exceed $5. Configure and verify an explicit net limit
before executing anything; target $0 initially, keeping the user's $5 unused.
Use bounded jobs, one GPU, no always-warm containers, and verify applicable
credit balance. Tax/authorization-hold behavior was not verified. Do not claim
signup alone guarantees no card activity. Environment budgets require paid tiers;
Workspace controls are the relevant ones for Starter.
Sources: https://modal.com/docs/guide/billing
https://modal.com/docs/guide/budgets

2. Kaggle — recurring free notebook research
Official usage guide lists a weekly quota of 30 hours, potentially higher with
available resources. Four weekly allowances would be 120 hours arithmetically,
not guaranteed capacity or a monthly reservation. Notebook documentation gives
12-hour CPU/GPU sessions and 20 GB auto-saved output storage. Check actual account
quota and available hardware before planning jobs.
A newer staff announcement retires P100 on September 15, 2026 and identifies
T4 x2 as the replacement: two GPUs, each with 16 GB. Older general documentation
still mentions P100, so that older hardware claim must not guide provisioning.
Two 16 GB cards are not automatically one 32 GB device: sharding needs compatible
software. Use for small speech/text quality tests. Large-model fit and modern
CUDA kernel compatibility need checking. Queuing and interruptions apply.
Sources: https://www.kaggle.com/docs/efficient-gpu-usage
https://www.kaggle.com/docs/notebooks
https://www.kaggle.com/product-announcements/735239

3. Hugging Face ZeroGPU — short free tests on larger GPU slices
Current docs: shared RTX Pro 6000 Blackwell, 48 GB default or 96 GB at twice quota.
Free accounts have five GPU minutes daily. That is 2.5 hours across 30 daily
allowances if used each day, not a bank of continuous time. Queues apply.
Existing Spaces can be tried free. Hosting up to two requires a verified-email
personal account older than 30 days and in good standing. Gradio-only with
PyTorch compatibility limitations; not a general-purpose always-on CUDA VM.
Source: https://huggingface.co/docs/hub/en/spaces-zerogpu

4. Lightning AI — useful first-month trial, not verified recurring free credits
Current pricing FAQ offers five credits on registration, 25 more after adding
a card, expiring after 12 months if unused. The advertised up-to-80 GPU hours
depend on GPU and interruptible pricing; do not promise that for L40S/A100.
The free 24/7 Studio is CPU, not GPU. Current pricing lists 10 GB free storage
and a 200 GB limit, which is not 200 GB of free storage. Phone verification is
used. Search snippets and an API subdomain showed differing prices; use the
current account quote instead of treating cached snippets as a purchase offer.
Source: https://lightning.ai/pricing

5. Google Colab Free — fallback
Free GPU access is possible, but exact hardware and usage allowance are dynamic
and not guaranteed. Free notebook runs last at most 12 hours, often less depending
on usage and availability. Use interactive notebooks, not a production serving
endpoint; remote SSH and bypassing its UI are restricted for free runtimes.
Existing eligible paid Google AI plans may include Colab benefits, but no such
subscription was verified for this user. Do not purchase a plan on assumption.
Source: https://research.google.com/colaboratory/faq.html

Other options checked
- AWS SageMaker Studio Lab: free for existing customers but closed to NEW
  customers. Existing users can use up to four GPU hours per 24 hours. Not a
  signup recommendation unless the user already has an eligible account.
  https://docs.aws.amazon.com/sagemaker/latest/dg/studio-lab.html
  https://docs.aws.amazon.com/sagemaker/latest/dg/studio-lab-overview.html
- Paperspace: free notebook M4000 is listed, but paid Pro starts at $8/month,
  over this budget. Free plan includes 5 GB storage; low priority for these models.
  https://docs.digitalocean.com/products/paperspace/pricing/
- Vast.ai: $5 minimum to start can fund burst rental. Storage still costs money
  when stopped; inbound/outbound transfer also costs money at host-specific rates.
  Zero balance stops, not destroys, instances; negative balances can be charged
  to the card. Therefore a $5 deposit is NOT an automatic total-spend guarantee.
  https://vast.ai/products/gpu-cloud
  https://github.com/vast-ai/docs/blob/main/guides/reference/faq/billing.mdx
- Runpod: $10 minimum card deposit excludes a fresh account under a strict $5
  cash budget even when a few GPU hours cost under $5.
  https://www.runpod.io/blog/manage-runpod-account-funding
- Lium: advertises $5 signup credit, expires in 14 days, one per person and daily
  grant availability conditions. Grants for research require individual approval.
  Useful bonus, not a verified recurring month-long allowance.
  https://lium.io/grants

Recommended month
Use Kaggle for repeated small-model/ASR tests and Modal for a few scheduled 48 GB
or larger GPU comparison jobs. If no payment method is acceptable, begin with
Kaggle; ZeroGPU is an optional brief-demo route for an eligible existing account.
Prepare fixtures and environments before allocating GPUs. Record model revisions,
precision, hardware and runtime separately. Test one model at a time; a 48 GB
GPU is not proof that every shortlisted model or precision fits. Larger BF16
models may require 80 GB or quantization, which changes the quality being tested.
Finish jobs, persist only reports/necessary weights within allowances, and release
compute. No credit or quota circumvention. Do not treat shared free-tier timings
as AWS production latency or evidence of a SOTA winner.
