# Find leads

A lead is a COMPANY that fits what the team sells and shows a public, dated
reason to talk now. Never a private person: do not look up, list or guess
anyone's personal email, phone, home address or social profile. Name the
role to approach (for example "Head of Operations"), not a person you found.

## 1. Know what the team sells, from its own context

Before searching anything, `recall` what the team sells, who it already sells
to, its prices, and any customer it won or lost and why. Write down, for
yourself, the fit in one line: industry, size, region, and the problem the
team solves. If the context points to one product or one market, go with it and
say what you assumed. Only if it says nothing about who the team sells to, ask
with `ask_first` now, before any search: once you have searched, you finish.

## 2. Search for signals, not lists

A list of "top 50 companies in X" is not a lead. Search for signals that a
company has the problem right now:

- hiring for the role that feels the problem (a job post),
- a funding round, an expansion, a new office, a new product line,
- a tool or vendor change the team replaces or plugs into,
- a public complaint, a tender, a regulation deadline that hits them.

Use specific queries (`"<industry>" "<region>" hiring "<role>"`,
`"<competitor> alternative"`, `"<industry>" raised Series A <year>`). Prefer
the company's own pages, job boards and reputable news over aggregators.

## 3. Check each one

Read the page the signal is on with `read_url` (or `read_site` for the
company's own site). Keep a company only when you read, yourself:

- what it does (one line, in your words),
- the signal, with its date,
- why it fits the team's line from step 1.

Drop any company whose signal is older than 12 months, that is already a
customer in the team's context, or that you could not read.

## 4. Answer

Five to ten companies, best first. For each, one short paragraph:

`<Company> (<site>): <what it does>. <The signal, with its date>. Fits because <reason from the team's context>. Approach: <role>, through <the company's public contact or careers page>.`

Then one line on what you searched and what you left out, so the team knows
how wide you looked. Save the list to the team's context with `remember`
only when the person asked for that.
