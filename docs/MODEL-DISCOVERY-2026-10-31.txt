The user has removed Latvian from the current launch requirements. Prioritise

Decision context
The user requires AWS self-hosting and excludes Apple models. Features include
streaming dictation, explicit voice editing, sentence correction, humanizing,
ordinary completion and remembered-fact sentence suggestions. Earlier answers
were too narrow: they omitted relevant recent releases and confused promising
candidates with a justified final selection. This document supersedes the
earlier shortlist as a research recommendation; it does not change app routing.

Method and limits
Discovery included recent web roundups, Reddit release posts and reports, model
catalogues, papers, model cards, and benchmark-owner pages. Community reports
were leads, not proof. Technical conclusions below use the original model or
evaluation sources. An Instagram-specific search did not identify the user's
particular post; no claim is made to have viewed it.
No new models were downloaded, no GPU resources launched, and no new inference
measurements performed in this research pass. Published latency and accuracy
are not Enkeon measurements. Dates refer to releases where explicitly stated,
not search indexing or latest file-update timestamps.

SPEECH: RECENT RELEASES AND RELEVANT BASELINES

1. NVIDIA Nemotron 3.5 Transcribe 30B-A4B, released October 28.
Offline audio model, approximately 4.3B active parameters including its encoder;
the 30B name is not a complete deployment-memory specification. Advertises 40
languages; its capability matrix includes Latvian. BF16, FP8 and NVFP4 variants
exist. NVFP4 requires Blackwell; its card reports a relative accuracy regression
versus BF16. Do not use BF16 benchmark scores to certify a quantized deployment.
Recommendation: add to the Latvian/contextual-ASR quality comparison against the
Latvian Whisper specialist. It is not a native streaming substitute.
Source: https://huggingface.co/nvidia/NVIDIA-Nemotron-3.5-Transcribe-30B-A4B-NVFP4

2. NVIDIA Nemotron 3.5 ASR Streaming 0.6B, June release.
Separate model from Transcribe. Cache-aware streaming with adjustable chunk
configuration. Its 40 language-locales comprise 19 transcription-ready, 13 broad
coverage and 8 adaptation-ready locales. Latvian is adaptation-ready and needs
fine-tuning, not advertised as usable production transcription out of the box.
80ms frames/chunks are not microphone-release-to-pasted-text latency.
Recommendation: first streaming candidate for supported languages; not an
unqualified Latvian solution. Governing licence listed as OpenMDW-1.1.
Source: https://huggingface.co/nvidia/nemotron-3.5-asr-streaming-0.6b

3. IBM Granite Speech 5.0 2B, October 20.
Apache-2.0 speech model with English, French, German, Spanish, Portuguese and
Japanese support. Includes contextual keyword and punctuation evaluations.
Recommendation: useful accuracy comparator, not Latvian coverage.
Source: https://huggingface.co/ibm-granite/granite-speech-5.0-2b

4. IBM Granite Speech 5.0 TurboCTC 470M, multilingual update October 21.
Seven explicitly listed languages: English, French, German, Spanish, Portuguese,
Italian and Japanese. The page metadata says eight; use the explicit support
list rather than inflating coverage. Non-autoregressive greedy decoding and
native punctuation/capitalization make it an interesting throughput candidate.
Do not equate non-autoregressive decoding with native streaming support.
Recommendation: lightweight ASR comparator; no Latvian claim. Apache-2.0.
Source: https://huggingface.co/ibm-granite/granite-speech-5.0-470m-turboctc

5. Qwen3-ASR 1.7B.
Public weights and streaming/offline toolkit; 30 languages plus 22 Chinese
dialects. Neither count means 52 distinct languages. Latvian is absent from the
explicit list. Recommendation: multilingual challenger to Nemotron streaming
and Voxtral, particularly for languages outside the latter's coverage.
Source: https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3-ASR-1.7B

8B stored parameters, 4B effective, based on Gemma 4 E4B. Supports direct

Recommendation: fast text-assistance challenger. Agentic/structured-output

12. EmbeddingGemma 2, October 6.
740M total, with selectively loadable components: 270M text, 170M vision, 300M
audio. Advertises 100+ languages, Apache-2.0. Recommendation: compare the text
path for fast memory lookup against current retrieval before considering any
index migration. An embedding is not a generated sentence or a truth checker.

13. Nemotron 3.5 Embed 1B, October 20.
Actually 1.55B including image encoder. Its text component was frozen and shares
the predecessor's embedding space; this release adds image retrieval. Therefore
newness alone is not evidence of improved text-memory retrieval. Metadata lists
34 languages; Latvian support not established here.
Recommendation: lower priority for today's text-only memory feature.
Source: https://huggingface.co/nvidia/Nemotron-3.5-Embed-1B-BF16

14. Qwen3 Embedding 0.6B and Reranker 0.6B.
Older Apache-2.0 baselines. The series advertises 100+ languages. Compare

BENCHMARK EVIDENCE: WHAT IT CAN AND CANNOT SETTLE
Open ASR and Artificial Analysis are useful independent evaluation sources.
Their public pages were inspected, but dynamic ranking data was not fully
available in the text extraction. No exact live rank is asserted here. AA's
non-streaming WER index and speed factor do not establish streaming end-to-end
latency or Latvian accuracy. Compare identical data, normalization and precision.
ErrataBench is a proofreading-specific evaluation by Revise, with seeded errors
in English documents. It is more relevant than coding benchmarks for correction,
but does not certify our shortlisted models' Latvian or memory performance.
Sources:
https://huggingface.co/spaces/hf-audio/open_asr_leaderboard
https://artificialanalysis.ai/speech-to-text/non-streaming
https://revise.io/blog/best-ai-model-for-proofreading

RECOMMENDED EXPERIMENT ORDER (engineering judgment, not published winners)
A. Streaming: Nemotron 3.5 ASR 0.6B vs Qwen3-ASR 1.7B vs Voxtral 4B.
B. Latvian: AiLab Whisper vs new Nemotron Transcribe. Fine-tuning streaming
   Nemotron is a separate project, requiring appropriate training data.
C. Immediate text suggestions/correction: Bolt E4B vs LFM2.5-2.6B vs Gemma4 12B.
D. Full rewrite/humanizer: Qwen3.8 27B vs Gemma4 12B vs Nanbeige4.3 33B;
   retain Qwen3.6 MoE as a speed control. Hemmingway only if commercial rights.
E. Memory retrieval: current Enkeon retrieval vs EmbeddingGemma2 text vs Qwen3
   Embedding0.6B, optionally Qwen3 Reranker0.6B. Reindex into a versioned parallel
   index; never mix incompatible embedding spaces in one index.
These are evaluation pools, not a plan to run every model for every request.

PRODUCT CONTRACT AND ACCEPTANCE TESTS
Dictation must preserve negations, names, dates, amounts and commands. Test
silence, music, noise, accents, mixed English/Latvian and private vocabulary.
Formatting may change presentation but not invent content. Voice edits require
an explicit edit gesture; failures preserve the original and permit recovery.
Correction should be minimal; humanizer must preserve facts while changing tone.
Use blinded human preference and fidelity evaluation, not AI-detector scores.

Memory: hybrid lexical/semantic retrieval, current-state and identity filters,
optional reranking, then a short source-constrained suggestion. Test old/new
conflicts, near-identical people, changed dates, unrelated memories, no results,
and instructions embedded in retrieved text. Include correct abstentions.
Source IDs alone do not prove a generated sentence is supported. If semantic
fidelity is not reliable, retain extractive suggestions instead of asserting
that a model makes paraphrases hallucination-free.

All suggestion requests must cancel on typing/focus changes and recheck cursor,
source text and destination before display or acceptance. Retrieval and generation
need independent latency measurements; a language model's large context is not
persistent personal memory.

AWS measurements must include network, queue, model execution, validation and
insertion; report p50/p95/p99, cold/warm behaviour, concurrency, failure rate,
GPU memory, and cost per audio minute or accepted suggestion. Keep short-context
workers warm. Separate heavy rewrites from suggestion traffic to avoid queueing.
Pin model revision, licence, precision, serving image, prompt and fixture hashes.
Do not choose an NVFP4 artifact for an incompatible GPU. Do not use active
parameter count to estimate stored weights. No hardware or cost promise yet.

Conclusion
The broader research materially improves the shortlist, especially October ASR,
small text models and memory retrieval. It does not establish a global SOTA
system. Selection remains contingent on our feature-specific AWS evaluation.
No Apple fallback is part of the proposed architecture; the installed app has
not been migrated by this research-only change.
