Floating bar positioning and interaction — 2026-11-01

Approved behavior: horizontally centred speaking orb, raised clear of the screen
edge, adaptive to display geometry; idle bar should not interfere with timelines.

Implementation
- Uses NSScreen.visibleFrame in points, accounting for reserved Dock/menu areas;
  extends to the screen bottom when no visible bottom Dock occupies that space.
- Active orb bottom clearance: 2% of usable height, clamped to 16–24 points.
- Removes the speaking orb's outer shadow to avoid a clipped line underneath.
- Prefers the frontmost application's front window's display when recording starts;
  otherwise uses pointer display. Keeps that display through recording/results.
- Repositions for display/Space changes; falls back safely if a display disappears.
- Idle activation is an 84x28-point bottom-centre zone on each display with 400ms
  dwell. Mouse-down/drag and scroll cancel hover activation.
- SwiftUI reports actual control bounds. Window ignores mouse events outside those
  bounds, and during drags started in another window. Recording orb/notice/tooltip
  have no click region; hands-free buttons and recovery card remain operable.
- Observer tokens and hover timer are cleaned up when the panel is destroyed.

Validation
App build and existing regression suite pass. New geometry checks cover laptop,
portrait, negative-coordinate/offset monitor and side-Dock usable rectangles.
Checks require centred placement inside usable bounds and 16–24-point clearance.
Hit-region checks cover visible control, transparent space, hidden controls and
drag-through. Actual multi-monitor hardware and cross-app video timeline gestures
were not exercised automatically; these checks validate layout and gating logic.
